# LifeOS product overview

LifeOS is a personal operating system: one place to plan the day, keep habits, pursue goals, develop skills and understand how life is going.

## Questions it should answer
What should I do today? Am I progressing towards my goals? Am I consistent? Where does my time go? What am I learning and becoming capable of? What am I neglecting?

## Principles
- Each module owns its data; analytics aggregate it and never copy it.
- Tasks are obligations. Side Quests are capabilities being developed. They are separate on purpose.
- Saving or importing a resource is collection, not learning. Only learning sessions count as learning effort.
- Structured and exploratory learning are both first-class; exploratory quests never get a forced percentage.
- Users control their data and integrations; every integration can be disconnected.
- Every visible control works; empty states are honest; no fabricated data.

## Modules
Home · Tasks · Habits · Goals · Daily planner · Calendar · Side Quests · Learning sessions · Resource Library · Knowledge vault · Imports (Chrome, YouTube, X) · Physical · Life Analytics · Settings.
